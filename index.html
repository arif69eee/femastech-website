What we do

Six electrical disciplines, one accountable team

Design, build, verify, and keep it safe. Most clients come to us for one of these and stay for the rest - because the drawings, the installation and the test results all come from the same engineers.

01

Electrical Consultancy

Full electrical design for a building or plant - ELD, SLD, load calculation, lighting simulation, LPS, earthing, solar and low-voltage systems - issued as a coordinated, approvable drawing set.

Details
02

Electrical Installation

Installation and site supervision to the approved design: substation and panel work, cabling and containment, earthing, LPS and low-voltage systems, handed over with as-built records.

Details
03

Electrical Inspection

Independent inspection of installed electrical work against BNBC, NEC and NFPA - what is compliant, what is not, and exactly what it takes to close each gap.

Details
04

Electrical Safety Audit

A structured audit of an operating facility: protection coordination, earthing integrity, panel condition, load balance and documentation, with a prioritised remediation plan.

Details
05

Electrical Tests

Instrumented on-site testing - thermographic scan, insulation resistance, earth resistance, LUX and transformer oil analysis - reported with measured values against limits.

Details
06

Electrical Training

Electrical safety and systems training for engineers, technicians and maintenance teams, delivered in-house through courses, workshops and seminars.

Details
