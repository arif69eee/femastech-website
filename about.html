About us

What we are

We are professionals engaged successfully in Bangladesh since 2014 in the field of electro-mechanical engineering services. We specialise in total mechanical work — fire and life safety, air line, steam line and HVAC — alongside electrical and plumbing services, basic and advanced.

We have completed more than 500 projects in Bangladesh across the RMG, residential, industrial, commercial, institutional and hospital sectors, and we have completed a LEED-certified factory. Our designs are produced to the guidelines of BNBC, NFPA, NEC, RAJUK, Bangladesh Fire Service & Civil Defense, ASHRAE and the RSC code of practice.

FEMAS Tech ensures customer satisfaction to fulfil our commitment. We are well decorated and committed as a consultancy firm in Bangladesh — qualified engineers, site engineers, business development personnel and AutoCAD draftsmen are all available in-house.

Vision

Where we are going

  • To provide the optimum solution in electro-mechanical engineering consultancy, and to create a positive attitude among clients and the community.
  • To contribute to the sustainable development of residential, educational, health care, assembly, commercial and industrial buildings by providing an overall solution of engineering, consultancy and management.
  • To create a skilled electro-mechanical engineering community through subject-specific training, workshops and seminars.
  • To be the associate of choice for the country's leading organisations in designing, building, assessing, testing and fitting out their requirements.
